Welcome to ASSESS DHT

What is this tool?

The Evidence Generation Support Tool helps digital health technology (DHT) developers identify which Health Technology Assessment (HTA) evidence recommendations apply to their specific product.

⚠
Please note: The ASSESS DHT Taxonomy focuses on medical devices. Therefore, this tool may not be appropriate for developers whose technology is purely administrative (e.g., medical scribing tools, basic EHR systems, or practice management software).

How to use it

  • Step 1: Technology Profile — Enter the specific characteristics, intended purpose, and functionalities of your DHT.
  • Step 2: Evidence Report — The tool filters through our framework to generate a customised list of evidence recommendations that apply to your technology, grouped by HTA domain.
  • Step 3: Evidence Planning — Review the generated recommendations, track your progress (Met, Uncertain, Not Met), and export a PDF plan to guide your evidence generation strategy.

How to use the recommendations

These recommendations represent best practices and common expectations from European HTA bodies. They should be used to guide your clinical study designs, data collection strategies, and regulatory evidence gathering.

Please note: This is a support tool and compliance with these recommendations does not guarantee regulatory approval or HTA acceptance.

A PICO framework (Population, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome) is a structured tool used to define the research question underpinning a clinical study or HTA submission. User Guide & Disclaimers

Instructions on how to navigate the ASSESS DHT Evidence Generation Support Tool and important information regarding its use.

How to Use the Tool

Step 1: Technology Profile
Begin by defining the core characteristics of your digital health technology (DHT). Accurately selecting your technology's purpose, hardware/software status, and AI capabilities ensures the filtering engine provides the correct recommendations. You can optionally filter by specific HTA domains or generate a PICO framework.

Step 2: Evidence Generation Report
The tool cross-references your profile against our embedded library of European HTA expectations. Review the recommendations grouped by domain. Expand each requirement to read detailed explanatory notes. You can export this raw report to PDF or proceed to the planning stage.

Step 3: Evidence Planning
Use this interactive workspace to conduct a gap analysis of your existing clinical and technical evidence. Mark items as 'Met', 'Uncertain', or 'Not Met'. You can then generate a targeted PDF export (e.g., exporting only the 'Not Met' requirements to share with your clinical research team).

Data Privacy & Security FAQ

Is my technology data safe and private?

Yes, your data is completely secure and private.

The ASSESS DHT Evidence Generation Support Tool is designed with a "client-side only" architecture, meaning all data processing happens locally within your own web browser. You can safely input sensitive or proprietary details about your digital health technology without risking exposure, data leaks, or third-party tracking.

The ASSESS DHT EGST has:

  • No Cloud Storage: We do not have a backend database. Your technology profile, PICO framework details, and evidence planning statuses are never transmitted to, or stored on, any external servers.
  • Local PDF Generation: When you click "Export PDF", the document is generated entirely by your browser, not by a remote server processing your inputs.
  • No Persistent Tracking: Because nothing is saved externally, all of your entered data is permanently erased the moment you close your tab or refresh the page, therefore please ensure you export your PDF plan before leaving the tool!
Disclaimers:
  • Not Regulatory Advice: This tool is intended for educational and strategic planning purposes only. It does not constitute formal legal, regulatory, or clinical advice.
  • No Guarantee of Approval: Compliance with the recommendations generated by this tool does not guarantee that your DHT will achieve regulatory certification (e.g., CE marking) or positive reimbursement decisions from national HTA bodies.
  • Evolving Standards: European HTA standards and medical device regulations (MDR/IVDR) are continuously evolving. While this library is periodically updated, you must always consult the latest official guidelines for your target jurisdictions.
  • Data Privacy: This tool operates entirely within your web browser. No data entered into the Technology Profile or Evidence Planning sections is saved to any external servers or databases. If you refresh or close the page, your progress will be lost unless you export it to PDF.
